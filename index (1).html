Content structure

The slow narrative framework

Click each stage to expand full guidance for your team.

1

0 – 10 sec

The hook

Name the emotional pain point immediately.

Open with a line that mirrors what your ideal guest feels: "People often arrive a little tired without fully realising it."

Identify the exact trigger — burnout, overstimulation, disconnection. Viewers who hear themselves will stay.

▾
2

10 – 45 sec

The immersion

ASMR, slow pans, rhythmic editing.

3–5 second shots. No jump cuts. Sound design carries the scene — birds, terracotta on stone, linen rustling.

Goal: a felt shift in the viewer's nervous system before any CTA. They should exhale before seeing a price.

▾
3

45 – 60 sec

The shift

Introduce the environment that enables change.

Show what changes when someone arrives. Villa, morning light, communal table. Make the environment feel like a character.

Text overlay at 45s: "Spaces for May are filling…" — scarcity planted gently before the CTA.

▾
4

60+ sec

The call to action

Clear, warm, specific. Monetisation happens here.

Don't break the spell with hard-sell language. Invite: "Comment RETREAT and we'll send the details."

Ensure your auto-response includes a direct booking link or beautifully formatted PDF brochure.

▾
